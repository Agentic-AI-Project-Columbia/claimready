# CPLR § 5004 — Statutory Pre-Judgment Interest Rate

Interest on a sum awarded for breach of contract accrues at the **rate of
nine percent (9%) per annum**, computed from the date the cause of action
accrued (i.e., the date of breach) through the date of judgment.

For unpaid services in a small-claims breach-of-contract case, the plaintiff
may demand:

  total = principal + (principal × 0.09 × days_since_breach / 365)

Interest is **separately recoverable** above the $10,000 monetary cap of
NYC CCA § 1805 — the cap applies to the principal only.

**Citation:** N.Y. C.P.L.R. § 5004.
