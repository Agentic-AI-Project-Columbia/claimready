**STATEMENT OF CLAIM**

1. On or about January 15, 2025, Plaintiff and Defendant entered into a
written agreement under which Plaintiff would provide graphic-design services
for Defendant's product launch.

2. The agreed contract price was $4,800.00, payable within thirty (30) days
of delivery.

3. Plaintiff performed all services and delivered final design files on
March 1, 2025. Defendant accepted the deliverables without objection.

4. Payment was due on March 31, 2025. Defendant failed and has continued to
refuse to pay any portion of the amount due.

5. On April 15, 2025, Plaintiff sent a written demand for payment.
Defendant did not respond.

6. Plaintiff has been damaged in the amount of **$4,800.00**, plus
statutory interest at 9% per annum from March 31, 2025 (CPLR § 5004).

**WHEREFORE**, Plaintiff demands judgment against Defendant in the amount of
$4,800.00 plus interest from March 31, 2025, plus the costs and disbursements
of this action.
