# How to File a Small Claim in NYC

A small claim is started by filing a **Statement of Claim** (form CIV-SC-50)
with the clerk of the NYC Civil Court Small Claims Part in the proper borough.

**Filing fee:** $20.00 for claims up to $1,000; $25.00 for claims from
$1,000.01 to $10,000.00. (Fees may be waived by motion if the plaintiff
qualifies as a poor person.)

**Required information:**
- Plaintiff's full name, address, daytime telephone, email
- Defendant's full legal name (for an LLC: the exact entity name as filed
  with the NY Department of State) and **service-of-process address** (for an
  LLC: the address on file with the Department of State)
- Brief plain-language statement of the claim (e.g., "unpaid invoice for
  graphic-design services rendered between Jan and March 2025")
- Amount of the claim (principal only; interest is computed by the court)
- Place where the claim arose

**After filing:** The clerk issues a Notice of Claim and serves it on the
defendant by certified mail at the service-of-process address. A trial date
is typically set within **45 days**.

**At trial:** The plaintiff brings all original evidence (contract, invoices,
emails, screenshots, the demand letter). The judge or arbitrator decides
informally; lawyers are permitted but not required.

**Citation:** NYC Small Claims Court Guide; CIV-SC-50 instructions.
