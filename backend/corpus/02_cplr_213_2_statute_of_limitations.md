# CPLR § 213(2) — Statute of Limitations for Contract Actions

Under New York Civil Practice Law and Rules § 213(2), an action **upon a
contractual obligation or liability, express or implied** must be commenced
within **six (6) years**.

The clock starts on the **date of the breach** — i.e., the date the
defendant first failed to perform (e.g., the date payment was due and not
made), not the date the contract was signed.

For unpaid services, the breach typically occurs the day after the agreed
payment-due date passes without payment.

If the cause of action accrued more than six years before filing, the claim
is time-barred regardless of merits.

**Citation:** N.Y. C.P.L.R. § 213(2).
