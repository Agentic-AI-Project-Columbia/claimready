# Venue — NYC Small Claims Part

A small claim may be brought in the NYC Civil Court Small Claims Part of the
borough where **any one** of the following is true:

1. The **defendant resides** (for individuals), or
2. The **defendant has a place of business** (for entities), or
3. The **defendant's employment** is located, or
4. The **transaction or occurrence** giving rise to the claim took place.

For a breach-of-contract claim against an LLC for unpaid services, the
plaintiff typically files in the borough where:

- The LLC's principal NYC place of business is located, **or**
- The services were performed, **or**
- The contract was signed.

If multiple boroughs qualify, the plaintiff may choose. The choice should
generally favor the borough most convenient for the plaintiff and most
clearly tied to the transaction.

**All defendants must have an address in New York City** for the case to
proceed in the NYC Small Claims Part. If the defendant is a foreign LLC
without NYC presence, the case must be filed elsewhere.

**Citation:** NYC Civil Court Act § 1801; NYC Small Claims Court Guide.
