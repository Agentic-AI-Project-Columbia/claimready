# NYC Civil Court Act § 1805 — Monetary Jurisdiction of Small Claims Part

The New York City Civil Court Small Claims Part has monetary jurisdiction over
any cause of action for money only **not in excess of ten thousand dollars
($10,000)**, exclusive of interest and costs.

A claimant **may not split a single cause of action** into two or more claims
to keep each under the cap. A claim above $10,000 must be brought in the
regular Civil Part of the Civil Court (or in another court of competent
jurisdiction).

This cap applies to all five boroughs of New York City (Manhattan, Bronx,
Brooklyn, Queens, Staten Island). Outside the City, the cap under the Uniform
City Court Act and Uniform Justice Court Act is generally $5,000.

**Citation:** NYC Civil Court Act § 1805.
